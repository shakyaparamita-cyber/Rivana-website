How to read a Fabric Note

FabricThe fiber content, by percentage.
MillWhere the fabric itself is woven and printed.
WeightMeasured in GSM — grams per square metre. Higher generally means heavier, denser fabric.
CertificationIndependent verification of what the fabric does, and doesn't, contain.

About

Made for softer evenings.

RIVANA makes sleepwear built around two things: cotton worth naming the mill for, and construction — ruffles, ties, hems — worth explaining rather than just showing.

What we believe

  • Transparency over hype. Every product page names the fabric, the mill, and the fit.
  • Fewer, better things. A short catalogue, done properly, beats a large one done quickly.
  • Comfort you can verify. Fabric weight and composition, not adjectives.

The story

RIVANA started with a fairly ordinary complaint: most sleepwear either overpromises — "luxury," "premium," "indulgent" — without saying what it's actually made of, or treats cotton basics as an afterthought category with nothing to say for itself.
